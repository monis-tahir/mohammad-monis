06 / ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

Good technology is appropriate technology.

Complexity is a cost. Introduce it only when the problem justifies it.

01

Understand the operational problem before selecting technology.

02

Prefer simple, secure and maintainable architectures.

03

Use deterministic systems where deterministic systems are sufficient.

04

Apply AI where uncertainty, language or pattern recognition creates real value.

05

Connect engineering decisions to business outcomes.
